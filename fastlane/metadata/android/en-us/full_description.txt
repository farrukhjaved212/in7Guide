IN7 Guide is an informational Android application built with Flutter, providing users with an easy way to explore organized guides, helpful information, and safety resources.

Features:

• Home: Access the application's main content through a simple interface.

• Explore: Browse available guides and informational articles.

• Search: Find relevant information quickly.

• Bookmarks: Save useful articles for easy access later.

• Safety: Read safety-related information and guidance.

• Simple Interface: Enjoy a clean and easy-to-use native Flutter experience.

IN7 Guide is designed to make informational content accessible through a lightweight mobile application.

The application is developed using Flutter and Dart.
